The current 13-step flow

1
"Donate to KCRW" headline — charity framing before membership is mentioned copy
2
Mailing address + login note above the fold — first thing visitors see after the headline UX
3
Charity Navigator badge — trust signal in the wrong place; belongs at point of payment UX
4
Three equal tabs: One-time / Monthly / Gift — monthly not pre-selected; all three equal visual weight conversion
5
Amount selector — $75 minimum for one-time; industry standard starts at $35–50 conversion
6
Membership tier descriptions — appear below the amount selector instead of above it; visitors choose price before seeing what they get UX
7
Gift selection wall: 20+ options, default is "Yes I want a gift" — psychology of choice research shows abandonment rises sharply above 5 options conversion
8
Billing form: 10+ fields — includes middle name, phone, full mailing address, 200-country dropdown. Digital membership needs: name, email, card friction
9
Shipping form — same fields again if a gift is selected; only relevant for physical fulfillment friction
10
Payment method selector — no Apple Pay or Google Pay; card-only on a page where 50.4% of visitors are on mobile friction
11
Processing fee upsell — framed as a deduction, not a collaboration; creates hesitation at the final step copy
12
Order summary — another review step before payment friction
13
Donate button — after 12 prior decision points, on a page loading in 15.8 seconds on mobile final

07 · Hypothesis & Expected Impact

Three hypotheses.
Each testable within 30 days.

Specific, measurable predictions based on industry research and publicly available data. Each can be confirmed or refined against KCRW's internal data.

01
Fixing join.kcrw.com performance and simplifying to a 3-step checkout will directly increase pledge drive conversions
At 13 steps with a 15.8s mobile load time and 50.4% mobile visitors, compounding friction is eliminating the vast majority of conversion-ready visitors before they reach the donate button. Google/Deloitte research shows ~20% conversion drop per additional 3 seconds of mobile load time. Reducing steps from 13 to 3 and load time from 15.8s to under 4s addresses both vectors simultaneously.
Expected outcome: 10–25% improvement in mobile conversion rate during the pledge drive. Measurable within 7 days via GA. PageSpeed May 15, 2026; Google/Deloitte mobile research
02
Converting newsletter CTAs from image-based to HTML text with benefits-driven copy will increase click-to-join rates
Image-based CTAs are blocked by default in a significant portion of email clients — meaning a portion of the 44% who open KCRW newsletters never see the membership ask. "Protect What You Trust. Donate Now." is pledge drive language, not a benefits-driven ask. A text-based CTA — "Early access to Summer Nights. Nick's texts. Film presales." — is visible in all clients, A/B testable without design changes, and personalizable by content affinity in HubSpot.
Expected outcome: At 0.5% click-to-join improvement on 5 Things (220K), ~1,100 new members/year. Measurable within 4 weeks. Live review May 2026; HubSpot benchmark data
03
Making monthly giving the default option will increase monthly donor proportion and double the LTV of each new cohort
Research consistently shows the default option in a donation flow captures 60–75% of completions. NPR and comparable public media organizations report 30–50% higher conversion rates when monthly is pre-selected. Monthly donors at $12–15/month carry approximately 2× the lifetime value of annual donors. This is a configuration change requiring no development work on most donation platforms.
Expected outcome: 20–40% shift toward monthly giving among new donors. Each monthly cohort ~$1,380+ LTV vs. ~$690 annual. Measurable within the current pledge drive cycle. Industry benchmarks; NPR member research
08 · Top 3 Priorities

Three moves.
This week. In this order.

Sequenced by urgency, lift required, and direct impact on the active pledge drive. All three can be implemented without new content, new audience, or significant budget.

1 — Simplify the checkout flow and fix mobile performance
⚡ Today — pledge drive live
Quick wins achievable in hours: pre-select monthly, remove middle name field, set no-gift as default, move match grant above the fold, enable Apple Pay / Google Pay via Stripe (already installed). Engineering sprint: reduce render-blocking JS (saves 4.4s), compress images (saves 1,306 KiB), defer unused JS (saves 1,150 KiB). Each improvement matters while the pledge drive is running.
Expected: 10–25% mobile conversion improvement. 50.4% of join page visitors are on mobile right now. PageSpeed May 15, 2026; BuiltWith May 2026
2 — Make monthly giving the pre-selected default
This week
Pre-select the monthly tab on join.kcrw.com, default amount to $10/month, and add a benefit callout at the $8+ tier ("Includes NPR+ ad-free"). Configuration change only — no development required on most donation platforms. Every donor acquired as monthly during this pledge drive carries approximately double the LTV of an annual donor.
Expected: 20–40% shift to monthly giving. Each monthly cohort ~$1,380 LTV vs. ~$690 annual. Industry benchmarks
3 — Convert newsletter CTAs to HTML text with benefits-led copy
This month
Update HubSpot newsletter templates to replace image CTAs with HTML text blocks and a styled button. Rewrite from "Protect What You Trust. Donate Now." to: "Early access to Summer Nights. Nick's texts. Film presales. That's what membership gets you. →" Text CTAs render in all email clients, can be A/B tested without design changes, and can be personalized by affinity — music subscribers hear about Masquerade; news subscribers hear about journalism access.
Expected: ~1,100 new members/year from 5 Things alone at 0.5% improvement. Live review May 2026; industry benchmarks
Not in this report: Welcome sequence, lapsed member win-back, event RSVP pipeline, paid search, affinity segmentation, platform consolidation. These represent $1.5M–$2.5M in addressable annual revenue — available in the full assessment.
